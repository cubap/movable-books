Birchwood League of Women Voters scrapbook — sachet extraction

A 1950s publicity scrapbook with a flap that opens to reveal a pocket containing a foil sachet of hand lotion. Drag the slider (or press Play) to move through the four states: flap closed → flap open → sachet extracted → sachet moved aside.

About this object & the states
  • 05 — Flap closed: the cover card sits over the pocket; the sachet is tucked inside.
  • 06 — Flap open: the “BALM ARGENTA” pocket and directions are revealed, sachet still in place.
  • 07 — The foil sachet is pulled out and draped over the top edge of the open flap card.
  • 08 — The sachet is moved aside and lies flat on the page (lower-right), flap closed again.
Photography recommendations
  • Lock camera position, exposure, and white balance across all states so the crossfade aligns without flicker.
  • Include a gray card or color target in at least one frame per setup.
  • For removable elements (like this sachet): photograph in place, mid-extraction, and fully removed on a plain, evenly-lit background at the same scale. A plain background enables a clean cutout (transparent PNG) for path-based animation later.
  • For flaps: capture each intermediate state (each flap individually, then combinations).
  • Keep the same resolution and aspect ratio across states; avoid re-cropping between shots.
