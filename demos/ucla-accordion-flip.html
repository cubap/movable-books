UCLA accordion fold and flip

An accordion-fold trade card from UCLA’s Patent Medicine Trade Cards collection. Use the slider to open or close the front, animate the fold, or flip the card to view the back. The back was photographed only in its collapsed state, so the card folds closed automatically before flipping over.

Fold pattern

The front of the trade card is divided into four vertical panels by three fold seams. When collapsed, panels 1 and 2 fold behind and panels 0 and 3 meet in front.

About this item

Original item: Alphonso & Imogene: an idyl of Henry's Carbolic Salve [inscribed] (ark:/21198/zz0002hh5q) , UCLA Digital Library.

The original record lists the item as a trade card published by John F. Henry & Co. of New York between 1870 and 1906, held by the Louise M. Darling Biomedical Library, History and Special Collections for the Sciences. The source rights statement is “public domain.”

The UCLA IIIF Manifest supplies three canvases: the front collapsed, the front fully expanded, and the back collapsed. This demo references the full-resolution IIIF Image resources directly rather than making local copies, and adds Web Annotation markup that describes the fold geometry non-destructively.
